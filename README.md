# Ropes & Recovery Gear

What a kinetic recovery (a snatch) puts through your snatch strap or kinetic rope and its shackles, which strap or rope suits your two vehicles, and what a safety lanyard, a tree protector and a bridle are each for. You set it up on an animated picture.

Every number comes from a standard (Australia's recovery strap standard), a maker (Bushranger, Factor 55, Samson, Crosby, WARN) or plain physics, and the Sources page shows each one with the words it comes from. Where nobody publishes a figure, the page says so.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript: open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing you enter leaves your browser.
